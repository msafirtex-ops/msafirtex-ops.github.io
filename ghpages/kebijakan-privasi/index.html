English

  • Scope. This policy covers the Safirtex website and the internal app "Tiktok Gbot".
  • TikTok integration. The app connects only to Safirtex's own TikTok account. Data accessed: basic profile (name, avatar, account ID), profile stats, video list and stats, and permission to upload/publish videos. It is used only to post Safirtex content and monitor its performance.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties for advertising. We do not access other TikTok users' data.
  • Service provider. The connection is managed through Composio (API connector); tokens are stored by that provider and used only for the purposes above.
  • Customers. Order data (name, WhatsApp number, shipping address) is used only to process orders.
  • Deletion. Access can be revoked anytime in TikTok settings; request data deletion by email.
